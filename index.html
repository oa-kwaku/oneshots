Race Effort HR Ranges

Enter your lactate threshold heart rate (LTHR) to get marathon, half marathon and 5K targets.

Find LTHR in Strava: Settings → My Performance → Heart Rate Zones, or take the average HR of the last 20 min of a 30-min all-out solo run (Friel test).

Race effort targets

Targets are typical ranges for a trained runner, as a percentage of LTHR. Start at the low end and let HR drift up late in the race. In the marathon, HR above the range before halfway is the main warning sign.
